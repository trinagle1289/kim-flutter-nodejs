# 時間評級

尋找可以幫助自動化、簡化或優化工作流程的技術解決方案

- 1\~2.5(5\~100)(Moderate)

  - 頻率尚可，請繼續保持。
  - The frequency is acceptable, please continue to maintain it.

- 3\~5(150\~500)(Slightly high)

  - 頻率較高，需看情況調整工時或穿戴護具。
  - With higher frequency, it may be necessary to adjust working hours or wear protective gear depending on the situation.

- 6\~10(750\~2500)(Excessively high)
  - 頻率過高，必須調整工時，或進行身體檢查。
  - With excessively high frequency, it is necessary to adjust working hours or undergo a physical examination.

# 負重

可以讓兩個人一起搬運，以降低單人負重，或將貨物分裝，以減少單件貨物的重量。
It can be carried by two people to reduce individual load, or the goods can be divided to lessen the weight of each item.

# 力量傳遞 / 負重條件

工作中應盡量平均地施力，防止其中一測施力過重造成危害

- In work, effort should be applied evenly to prevent excessive force on one side, which can cause harm.

# 身體姿勢

- 姿勢評級
  - 選擇不太費力的姿勢進行搬運
  - Choose less strenuous postures for lifting.

- 額外加分
  1.  保持軀幹穩定：嘗試通過轉動腳步而不是腰部來改變方向，這樣可以減少軀幹的扭轉和側傾。
  - • Maintain trunk stability: Turn with your feet, not your waist, to reduce trunk twisting.\n
  2.  保持物體靠近身體：搬運物品時，盡量讓物品靠近身體，避免過度伸展手臂。
  - • Keep objects close: Hold items close to your body to avoid overreaching.
  3.  規劃工作動線：重新規劃工作路徑和流程，盡量減少手臂抬舉的次數和時間。
  - • Plan workflow: Reorganize paths and processes to minimize arm lifting.
  4.  使用工具：讓你可以在較低的位置進行操作，減少手臂高過肩膀的情況。
  - • Use tools: Operate at lower positions to reduce overhead arm movements.

# 不良工作條件

1. 手/手臂的位置與動作
   - 調整工作姿勢，避免發生達到關節活動範圍極限的動作。
   - • Adjust your working posture to avoid movements that reach the limits of your joint range of motion.
2. 力量傳遞 / 應用受限
   - 使用防滑手套、安裝輔助工具、調整工作姿勢與高度。
   - • Use anti-slip gloves, install assistive tools, and adjust your working posture and height.
3. 不良的氣候條件
   - 熱或不舒服的吹風的狀況-可以安裝空調或風扇。
   - • For hot conditions, use air conditioners or fans; for cold or damp conditions, use insulation or waterproofing.
4. 寒冷或潮濕的狀況
   - 可以增加保暖或防水措施。
   - • Adjust the work process or rearrange the work area, and carry out site cleaning and maintenance.
5. 受限的空間條件
   - 調整工作流程或重新安排工作場地還有進行場地的清潔與維修。
6. 衣服
   - 可以選擇輕便舒適的防護裝備。
   - • You can choose lightweight and comfortable protective gear.
7. 搬運/握持困難
   - 增加人員協助或使用搬運車。
   - • Increase personnel assistance or use a hand truck.

# 工作協調 / 時間分佈

與主管或同事討論工作安排，聽取意見和建議，及時調整工作計劃。

- Discuss work plans with your supervisor or colleagues, listen to their advice, and adjust as needed.
